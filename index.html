Introduction

The details, made simple

What kinds of sessions can I book?

Portraits, couples, weddings, founder stories, editorial work, and small-brand campaigns. If it matters to you, it is worth photographing.

How do I reserve a date?

Send a note with your date, location, and the kind of story you are imagining. I reply within one business day with availability, a tailored package, and next steps.

Are digital files part of every package?

Yes. Your edited high-resolution gallery is included, with web-sized copies and print ordering available from the same private gallery.

Do you travel for commissions?

Absolutely. Travel is built into selected packages, and I am always happy to create a custom quote for a destination project.

Can I order prints or an album?

Yes. Fine art prints, framed pieces, linen albums, and small gift boxes can be added to any session or ordered later from your gallery.

Do you teach photography too?

Private portfolio reviews, one-to-one mentoring, and seasonal editing workshops are available for photographers building a more intentional practice.

Can you help with styling and planning?

Yes. Every commission starts with a visual direction call, and brand projects include shot lists, location notes, and light styling guidance.

When will my gallery be ready?

Portrait galleries arrive within two weeks. Weddings and larger campaigns take three to six weeks, with rush editing available by request.
