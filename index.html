EVENTS

Event photography is about capturing the magic of live occasions. Whether it's a wedding, corporate event, or milestone celebration, I'm there to document every heartfelt moment, from the big reveals to the quiet in-between glances that people forget happened.

Service Highlights

  • Coverage for weddings, parties, corporate functions, and more
  • Skilled photography that knows how to seize the moment
  • A mix of candid and posed shots for a comprehensive story
  • Quick turnaround so you can relive the day's highlights

PORTRAITS

Personal, editorial and professional portraits that feel natural and look sharp. We plan the look together, then I guide you through the session so you relax and the photographs show who you really are.

Service Highlights

  • Studio or on-location sessions
  • Lighting and styling direction included
  • Retouched final selects, ready for web and print
  • Headshots, family, couples and personal branding

PRODUCTS

Clean, detail-rich product and brand photography for shops, packaging and campaigns. Every shot is composed to show quality and make the product the hero.

Service Highlights

  • White-background and styled lifestyle shots
  • Consistent look across a whole catalog
  • Web-optimized and print-ready files
  • Fast delivery for launches and promotions

FAQs

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What type of photography do you specialize in?

I specialize in portrait, landscape, event and commercial photography, capturing memorable moments with a natural, editorial look.

How can I book a photography session with you?

Send a message through the contact section with your date, location and what you have in mind. I reply within one business day with availability and a quote.

What equipment do you use for your photography?

I shoot on professional mirrorless cameras with a range of prime and zoom lenses, plus portable lighting for studio and on-location work.

Can I request a specific location for a shoot?

Yes. I travel for shoots and can also suggest locations that suit the mood and style you want.

What is your editing process like?

I cull the best frames, then color-grade and retouch each selected photo so the final set looks consistent and polished.

Are digital files included in your photography packages?

Yes, every package includes high-resolution digital files delivered through a private online gallery.

Do you offer prints of your photographs?

Prints are available for purchase. Ask about the "Prints" option for sizes, paper types and pricing.

How long does it take to receive the edited photos after a session?

Most galleries are delivered within two weeks. Events and rush orders can be turned around faster on request.
